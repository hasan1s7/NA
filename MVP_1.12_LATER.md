# Later, not blockers for this MVP

Keep the current release centred on choosing content, contextual understanding, explicit saving, optional practice and durable return.

Not implemented here: arbitrary-site/video ingestion; native app blocking or an Opal integration; Midjourney or paid generation; a trained learner/recommendation model; universal morphology/semantic grading; new recording infrastructure; universal audio/video synchronization; global mastery scores; larger language catalogues; progress-history import/compaction; archived multi-version content publication.

Next priorities after local device checks: independent Arabic/editorial review; observe a handful of actual people choosing and finishing pieces; fix the biggest observed friction; decide whether catalogue quality or capture convenience is the next real constraint. Do not invent pilot users, quotes, learning gains or product-market fit.

Suggested exploratory test (not run): recruit 5-8 consenting adult learners, ask each to choose a piece independently, observe first meaningful explanation, optional save and recovery, then invite voluntary return. Report denominators: independent starts / enrolled, completed desired action / attempted, returns on another day / people invited. A small delayed unaided expression check measures that specific task, not general fluency; keep exposed, assisted, skipped and unaided evidence separate. Do not add third-party analytics or collect unrelated browsing.
