I’m a researcher and founder working on statistical methods and software for decisions under uncertainty. My work spans causal machine learning with dependent data, biobank-scale inference, educational measurement, and multilingual learning systems.

At Nabu, I bring those interests together: language structure, evidence about a learner, and the choice of what support to offer next.
